  Changes made to the original: the city list was filtered to administrative
  centres (plus US and Canadian cities above 100,000 inhabitants); a
  "continent" column was added by joining on country name and, for cities the
  join did not cover, by majority vote over the three nearest labelled cities;
  duplicate city names were collapsed to the most populous entry; and only the
  city, country, continent, lat, lon and population columns were retained.
